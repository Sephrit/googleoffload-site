Privacy policy

Offload Filter Controller is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own Gmail account.

  • It asks Google for read-only Gmail access. It cannot send, change or delete mail.
  • Mail it reads is stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Nothing is shared with, sold to, or sent to anyone else, and no data is used for advertising.
  • Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's security settings, under third-party connections.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
